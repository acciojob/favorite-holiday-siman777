Manali


Manali is a beautiful hill station located in Himachal Pradesh, India. It is famous for its snow-covered mountains, beautiful valleys, adventure activities, and peaceful environment.

  1. Solang Valley - Famous for adventure sports.
  2. Rohtang Pass - Known for snow-covered mountains.
  3. Hadimba Temple - A beautiful ancient temple.
  4. Old Manali - Famous for cafes and scenic views.
  5. Jogini Waterfall - A beautiful waterfall.

Must-Try Foods in Manali

  • Siddu - Traditional Himachali bread.
  • Dham - Traditional Himachali meal.
  • Thukpa - Tibetan noodle soup.
  • Momos - Steamed or fried dumplings.
  • Babru - Himachali stuffed bread.
